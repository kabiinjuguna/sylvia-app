Sylvia Voice Training – Full Conversation

How Conversation Training Works

1. Phrase you'll say – What you speak to trigger Sylvia.

2. Sylvia's voice response – What Sylvia speaks back to you (in her voice).

3. Action / Response – What Sylvia should do (open field – e.g., "create proforma", "send email", "calculate 10% of 5000", or any custom instruction).

4. Sylvia's response about the action – What Sylvia says after completing the action.

💡 All fields are open – you can type any command, instruction, or response. Sylvia will remember and execute the full conversation flow.
